How we make it: AI in this blog and in the studio's series

Plainly: AI is part of how both the series and these pages are made.

Checked
30 October 2026

AI is part of how both the studio's series and these pages are made. This page says what we can say about each, and where the line is.

The articles on this blog

An AI model drafts the articles. Every fact in an article has to come from a source that was opened and read, and the sources are listed under the article with the date they were checked. A claim that could not be confirmed is cut, not softened.

Before an article goes up, an automated check looks at it. It wants at least two sources, a direct answer in the first paragraph, and the question the article answers taken from what people really type into search engines. It also stops phrases this site does not use. We do not claim that a human editor has read every line.

The studio's series

The studio's press kit describes When Time Comes as an AI-first production in an oil-painting style. We do not describe the studio's tools or process beyond what its own site says, because that is all a reader can check.

What we do not do

We do not hide that AI is involved, and we do not write reviews, viewer numbers or quotes that nobody gave. When an article is about someone else's film or series, it relies on what that work's makers and published sources say.

Where to watch and follow

Nothing has aired yet. The studio's site has the series, and the Telegram channel says when the first episode is out.
